The connection between the video game characters and the trading cards blew the mind of 11-year-old me. I'm not sure I managed to hold onto any of these from my childhood to my adulthood, but I sure wish I had, because it reached an all-time high this week.
The Unlimited Gym Heroes printing of Sabrina's Gengar has reached a Market Price of $310. That's up from $290 at the beginning of the year, and double what it was a year ago, for context. What's striking about the climb on this card is that it's not so much of a spike—it eluded our automated price checks for most of 2025—but how it's just been a very slow, very steady climb upward for the card going back as far as 2022 (when it was just $75).
The truth is that this Gengar's price is running even higher than the leading number might indicate, because live listings show that the least expensive Near Mint Sabrina's Gengar is sitting at $390. That's big for the Trainer's Pokémon, and it turns out it's not even the only Sabrina's Gengar(!) climbing this week.

Two Gym Leader sets, two Sabrina's Gengar cards, two price spikes.
The Unlimited Gym Challenge printing of Sabrina's Gengar is less iconic, and less expensive. But it's been on its own upward climb over the last year, and after another jump over the last week it's up to a Market Price over $100. That's again a lagging indicator—you're looking at $185 on the current Marketplace for a Near Mint copy of the 26-year-old card. But hey, people, myself included, love Gengar. Heck, M Gengar-EX from Phantom Forces jumped in price just this week from $207 up to $220, and Sabrina's Gengar is one of the coolest Gengar cards there is.
Now onto something a lot more recent, and a different kind of cute.

A decent little bump for the standout Special Illustration Rare from Journey Together this week. Lillie's Clefairy ex 184 climbed this week from a Market Price of $117 up to $127. It's the first positive week in a long time for the only true standout from the set. Salamence ex 187 and N's Zoroark ex 185 both clear the $50 threshold, but nothing else does, and that's as close as anything comes to Clefairy.
It's been an up-and-down nine months since Clefairy's printing. It came along right in the biggest rush of 2025, and after coming out of the gates around $250 eventually went down as low as $125. It then caught up in the larger market push that saw the Pokémon TCG top out in October, and at that time Clefairy briefly went back up to $175 before dropping back down to $115 to set the stage for this week's gain.

Also up this week is one of my favorite pairings ever put to paper. This week we saw the SM Promo printing of Magikarp & Wailord climb from $72 up to $82 in price. The card has been on an absolute tear since the beginning of the year when it dipped as low as $68. For reference, it's coming in today at triple what it was a year ago.
It's not the only Magikarp & Wailord climbing in price. The Team Up Secret Rare printing of Magikarp & Wailord-GX reached an all-time high this week of $120 in price. That's up from $110 at the beginning of the year, and double what it was six months ago. The Full Art printing of Magikarp & Wailord-GX from the set recently reached an all-time high of just over $100 in November and has been hanging there ever since.
Before we move on from Magikarp, I'll never miss a chance to check on the Paldea Evolved printing of Magikarp jumping up a waterfall. After reaching a recent low of $250 at the end of December, the fish has made at least a bit of a rebound, climbing up to $270 since that time.

Team Up really did serve up some excellent pairings, and Eevee & Snorlax-GX certainly qualifies as one of those. I'm a big fan of this duo, and it turns out I'm not alone, because the Secret Rare printing of Eevee & Snorlax-GX has started to climb again. It's up from $220 to $235 over the last week.

There's just a hint of movement on this Ultra Rare printing of Mewtwo in the Crown Zenith: Galarian Gallery release: in the past two weeks it's climbed from a recent low of $157, up to $175. Not quite an all-time high for the Psychic powerhouse (it briefly reached over $200 back in October), but still a big week for Mewtwo VSTAR.
A little further and Mewtwo will be on the heels of the set's most expensive card: Giratina VSTAR GG69. The unmistakable art makes it very recognizable, and after touching down as low as $175 back at the end of 2025, it's up this week to a Market Price of around $200. Giratina has also been climbing of late, moving up from $175 at the end of the year to $205 today. The final of the big three cards in that set is Arceus VSTAR GG70, which too is showing momentum this month as it climbed from $108 to $118 over the last few weeks.

Lastly this week, let's take a look at one of the most aggressive climbers of all of 2025, at it again in the new year. Lugia-EX 94 from 2015's Ancient Origins was never bulk, but it was never really the Lugia everyone talked about owning over the last few years. But the price has been going crazy, and it's hard to believe that the Lugia that could be abundantly found around the $75 mark a year ago is now sitting at $300 on live listings for a copy.
